Who We Are

Passionate About Sri Lanka's Wonders

Ceylon Escapes was founded in 2016 by a team of local travel enthusiasts who believed the best stories of Sri Lanka weren't in any guidebook — they were shared over a cup of freshly-picked hill-country tea, or whispered by a guide at the foot of Sigiriya as the sun rises.

We started with a single van and a deep passion for showing visitors the real Sri Lanka — beyond the postcard images, into the living, breathing culture, cuisine, and landscape that makes this island utterly unique.

Today, we operate over 15 curated tour packages and have welcomed more than 2,000 travellers from across the globe, all while maintaining the personal, attentive service of a small family-run operation.
