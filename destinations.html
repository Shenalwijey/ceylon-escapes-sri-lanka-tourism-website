Explore Sri Lanka

From misty highland peaks and ancient kingdoms to pristine coastlines and wild national parks — 12 extraordinary destinations await.

Sigiriya Rock Fortress

📍 Central Province  ·  ⭐ 4.9

A UNESCO World Heritage Site and one of Asia's greatest archaeological wonders — a 5th-century palace complex carved into a 200m volcanic rock rising above the jungle.

Kandy — Temple of the Tooth

📍 Kandy District  ·  ⭐ 4.8

Sri Lanka's last royal capital sits at the heart of the island. The sacred Temple of the Tooth Relic, the Royal Botanical Gardens, and vibrant Kandyan cultural shows await.

Highlands

Nuwara Eliya Tea Country

📍 Central Highlands  ·  ⭐ 4.7

Rolling emerald tea estates, cool misty air, colonial bungalows, and the world's finest Ceylon tea. A magical escape from the tropical heat of the lowlands.

Ella & Nine Arch Bridge

📍 Uva Province  ·  ⭐ 4.9

The iconic Nine Arch Bridge, Little Adam's Peak, and Ella Gap views make this highland village one of Sri Lanka's most photographed and beloved destinations.

Galle Fort

📍 Southern Province  ·  ⭐ 4.8

A remarkably preserved Dutch colonial fort on Sri Lanka's southern tip — labyrinthine cobblestone streets, boutique galleries, and sunset views over the Indian Ocean.

Mirissa Beach

📍 Southern Coast  ·  ⭐ 4.8

A postcard-perfect crescent bay, legendary for blue whale and spinner dolphin watching from November to April. Calm turquoise waters perfect for swimming.

Wildlife

Yala National Park

📍 Southern Province  ·  ⭐ 4.9

Home to the world's highest density of leopards — as well as elephants, sloth bears, crocodiles, and hundreds of bird species. A wildlife safari like no other.

Minneriya — The Gathering

📍 North Central Province  ·  ⭐ 4.9

Witness "The Gathering" — hundreds of wild elephants congregating around a vast ancient reservoir. One of Asia's most spectacular wildlife events, from July to October.

Trincomalee & Nilaveli

📍 Eastern Province  ·  ⭐ 4.7

Sri Lanka's hidden gem on the east coast — crystal clear waters ranked among the world's best for snorkelling and diving. Pigeon Island Marine National Park lies just offshore.

Sri Lanka's medieval capital — a UNESCO site with remarkably intact 12th-century palaces, dagobas, and the magnificent Gal Vihara rock-carved Buddha statues.
