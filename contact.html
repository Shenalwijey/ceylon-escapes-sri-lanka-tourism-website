FAQs

Common Questions

Quick answers to the questions we hear most often.

🗓️ When is the best time to visit Sri Lanka?

The west & south coasts are best from November to April; the east coast peaks from May to September. The cultural highlands are enjoyable year-round. We'll recommend timing based on your chosen destinations.

💳 How do I pay for my tour?

After booking confirmation we'll send an invoice. We accept bank transfer, credit/debit card, and PayPal. A 20% deposit secures your booking; the balance is due 14 days before departure.

🔄 What is your cancellation policy?

Free cancellation up to 14 days before departure. Within 7–14 days, a 25% fee applies. Cancellations within 7 days are non-refundable, but we'll always try to reschedule.

🧳 Are flights included?

International flights are not included — we recommend booking via Colombo (CMB) or Mattala (HRI). We're happy to advise on the best routes and can arrange airport pickup.

👨‍👩‍👧 Do you offer family-friendly tours?

Absolutely! We customise every itinerary for the group — slower pacing, child-friendly activities, family room accommodation, and guides experienced with children.

📋 Do I need a visa for Sri Lanka?

Most nationalities require an Electronic Travel Authorization (ETA), easily obtained online before travel. We'll provide full guidance and requirements for your passport nationality.
